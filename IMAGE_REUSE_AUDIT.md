## Summary

- **IMG_6763.PNG** is the most reused: peintre pro, team, blog/ressources “résidentielle”, and the **résidentielle** slot on all four city pages (and more).
- **Blog hub** images (IMG_6764, IMG_6763, IMG_6762, IMG_6753, etc.) are shared by: Blog page hero, Resources section, ServiceQuartierGuidesSection, and blog post data.
- **2-services** images (IMG_6760, IMG_6777, IMG_6755, etc.) are reused across main service pages, sous-service data, city pages, and blog post data.
- **3-ville** city pages each use their own folder, but **IMG_6763.PNG** (résidentielle) is the same file in Montréal, Laval, Brossard, and Longueuil.
- **new-landing** (method-1..4, control-1..3) is shared between MethodSection and ProcessStepsSection (method only).

If you want to reduce reuse, the main candidates are: **IMG_6763** (use a different “résidentielle” image per city or per section), **blog hub** set (different hero vs cards vs data), and **2-services** images that appear in both main service pages and sous-service/city/blog data.
